# Product

GF Anniversary: a private gift website Cristóbal built for Josefa to celebrate their relationship. Not a commercial product.

> Inferred from the repository and the owner's brief; no interview was run because the owner asked to proceed without questions. Items marked (assumed) are not confirmed.

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 14 (App Router), React 18, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS 3, Framer Motion. Frontend only, deployed to Cloud Run on every push to `main`.

## Users

- Josefa, the recipient. Opens it mostly on her phone (assumed), on the anniversary date and afterwards to revisit photos and songs.
- Cristóbal, the author, who maintains the content.

## Product Purpose

Let Josefa walk through nine years together: the real photos, the songs that matter to them, the story timeline and the personal notes Cristóbal wrote for each memory. Success is that it feels made for her, not assembled from a template.

## Positioning

A handmade keepsake. The content is the value: real photos, real dates, his own words in informal Chilean Spanish.

## Operating Context

- Relationship start: 25 November 2017. Ninth anniversary: 25 November 2026. First kiss: 25 September 2017.
- Josefa's birthday is 2 December; a birthday surprise appears only on that date.
- Music plays through a site-wide player while browsing.
- Private areas sit behind a simple client-side login.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sections: home, gallery (photos by category, a "love story" sequence, lightbox), music (15 tracks with a memory note each), about (timeline, day counter, stats), games, chat (placeholder), birthday (date-gated).
- 30 real photos and 15 mp3 files are the only media. No backend.
- All copy is in Spanish and written by Cristóbal; it must be preserved, not rewritten.
- Images of the couple must never be AI-generated.

## Brand Commitments

- The J&C monogram exists as the site icon.
- Owner rejected the previous look: Playfair Display and Dancing Script, the burgundy/pink/gold palette, gradients on every surface, and emoji used as illustration.
- Owner wants: real SVG illustration and icons, photographs as the lead, and animation kept as part of the experience.

## Evidence on Hand

30 photographs (New York, snow, a temple visit, Pixar/Up, Halloween, Fiestas Patrias, zoo, everyday meals), a 9-entry timeline, 15 songs with memory notes.

## Product Principles

1. The photographs and his words lead; interface chrome recedes.
2. Nothing generic: every label and ornament should belong to this couple's story.
3. Motion is part of the gift, but one moving thing at a time.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable on a phone in one hand; honors reduced-motion; audio never blocks reading.
